## Overview

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is a contract between Contabo and its customer that governs the processing of personal data by Contabo on behalf of the customer, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). *Personal data* means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person; *processing* covers operations such as collection, storage, retrieval and erasure.

A DPA is only mandatory under certain circumstances and only for certain natural or legal persons. Whether one is required for a given use is for the customer to determine (see the GDPR text and guidance at gdpr.eu).

## Creating a DPA

1. Log in to the Customer Control Panel.
2. Click **Create new Data Processing Agreement**.
3. Confirm the customer details.
4. Select the Contabo services the agreement should cover.
5. Optionally enter the contact details of a Data Protection Officer.
6. Specify the categories of personal data processed (e.g. address data, authentication data, bank details, billing data).
7. Specify the categories of affected persons (e.g. applicants, customers, employees).
8. Review the generated PDF preview.
9. Conclude the agreement by ticking the confirmation checkbox.

The concluded agreement can be downloaded from the DPA section at any time.
